How it works

03

We don't read every pixel — yet.
Why we're upfront: We fetch the page and its linked stylesheets server-side, then resolve the design tokens and component rules the site actually ships — including var() and calc() evaluated to concrete values. We don't run a headless browser, so anything that only exists after JavaScript paints is outside what we can see.

This works best for websites where the design is represented through standard HTML/CSS. Sites that render everything in JavaScript or hash their CSS at build time (Linear-style apps, canvas-heavy pages) give us far less to read, and we say so rather than hand you a thin spec that looks confident.
