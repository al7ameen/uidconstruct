How it works

01

Paste the URL

We inspect the site's available HTML/CSS signals and analyse its structure.

02

Extract the spec

We identify colors, typography, spacing, layout, responsive behaviour, and interaction patterns.

03

Paste into your editor

Use the generated specification with v0, Bolt, Cursor, or your preferred AI coding tool.

We don't read every pixel — yet.
Why we're upfront: We extract computed DOM styles — colors, spacing, typography, and layout rules — but we don't capture everything such as complex SVG paths or canvas animations yet.

This works best for websites where the design is represented through standard HTML/CSS. SVG & canvas are in private beta.
Currently optimised for HTML/CSS interfaces
