Use the tool fairly

uidconstruct is free while in beta. The free tier is limited to 10 analyses per hour per IP, enforced server-side. Do not use it as an automated scraper farm, resell the endpoint, or point it at URLs you are not permitted to access.

What the output is — and is not

We extract design values that a website's own public HTML and CSS already declare: colours, font sizes, spacing, radii, shadows. A specification is a description of a design, not a copy of it. It is a starting point for your own implementation.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for what you build from a spec. Design tokens themselves are generally not copyrightable, but reproducing a specific site's overall look can raise trade-dress and trademark issues, and logos, icons, photographs and copy are usually protected. Do not clone a site to pass it off as theirs. Use this to learn how a layout is built, or to keep your own design system consistent.

No warranty

The tool is provided "as is", with no guarantee of accuracy or availability. We parse HTML and CSS with a parser, not a browser, so anything that only exists after JavaScript paints may be missing or wrong. Specs are generated by a language model and can contain values it inferred rather than read. Verify before shipping.

Availability

This is a beta on a free tier. It may change, rate-limit, or disappear without notice. We do not promise uptime.
